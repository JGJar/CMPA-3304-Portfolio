My Top 5 Anime

  1. Ace of Diamond

    I love how the main character is always grinding to become the best. He enters a new environment and keeps working hard no matter what challenges he faces. Along the way, he makes friends who support him and help him grow.

  2. Bleach

    I think the writing and characters are the main reasons I love Bleach so much. It also has a really cool power system, with characters having unique abilities and transformations. On top of that, it has one of the best villains in anime history.

  3. Classroom of the Elite

    The main character is one of the smartest characters I have seen in anime. No matter who comes after him, he finds a way to deal with the threat so he can stay at the school. I also like how he manipulates other characters to get what he wants.

  4. Dr. Stone

    This is just a super fun science anime. The main character literally creates things from nothing using science in a world that has been turned to stone. I really like seeing how he uses his knowledge to slowly rebuild everything.

  5. Grand Blue

    This is the funniest anime I have ever seen. It is about a group of college students who join a diving club, but most of the time they are making jokes, drinking, and getting themselves into ridiculous situations.
